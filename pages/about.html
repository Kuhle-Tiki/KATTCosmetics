About KATT Cosmetics

Made by makeup artists who wanted better options.

Our History

KATT Cosmetics was founded in 2021 by a team of working makeup artists tired of choosing between salon-quality pigment and drugstore pricing. It started with a single hero product, a 24-shade liquid foundation range, before growing through word-of-mouth among freelance MUAs into a full range of face, eye and lip products.

Today the brand operates through pop-up counters, select retail partners and this website, which showcases our full range. All purchases are made directly through our team via WhatsApp, email and Instagram. See where to find us on our store locations map.

Our Mission

We believe that makeup should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their skin tone or budget. Our mission is to provide high-quality, affordable cosmetics that empower individuals to express themselves and feel confident in their own skin.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a leading cosmetics brand that is recognised for its commitment to inclusivity, sustainability, and innovation. We aim to create products that not only enhance beauty but also promote self-expression and creativity.

Our Values

  • Inclusivity: We celebrate diversity and strive to create products that cater to all skin tones and types.
  • Sustainability: We are committed to reducing our environmental impact through eco-friendly packaging and responsible sourcing.
  • Innovation: We continuously research and develop new products to meet the evolving needs of our customers.
  • Quality: We prioritise the quality of our products, ensuring they are safe, effective, and enjoyable to use.

Our promise

  • Cruelty-free formulas, always.
  • A shade range built for everyone.
  • Honest, professional-friendly pricing.

Who we're for

  1. Everyday beauty lovers

    Aged 18-50, budget-conscious but quality-focused, active on social media, always up for a new tutorial or trend. Browse our makeup range.

  2. Freelance and salaried makeup artists

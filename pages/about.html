About KATT Cosmetics

Made by makeup artists who wanted better options.

Our History

KATT Cosmetics was founded in 2021 by a team of working makeup artistes tired by chooseing between salon quality pigment and drugstore pricing. it started with a single hero product - a 24-shade liquid foundation rage - before growing through word-of-mouth freelance MUAs into full range of face, eye and lip products.

Today the brand operates through pop-up counters, select retail partners and this website - showcases for our full range , with all purchases made directy thrugh our team via WhatsApp, email and instagram.

Our Mission

We believe that makeup should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their skin tone or budget. Our mission is to provide high-quality, affordable cosmetics that empower individuals to express themselves and feel confident in their own skin.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a leading cosmetics brand that is recognized for its commitment to inclusivity, sustainability, and innovation. We aim to create products that not only enhance beauty but also promote self-expression and creativity.

Our Values

  • Inclusivity: We celebrate diversity and strive to create products that cater to all skin tones and types.
  • Sustainability: We are committed to reducing our environmental impact through eco-friendly packaging and responsible sourcing.
  • Innovation: We continuously research and develop new products to meet the evolving needs of our customers.
  • Quality: We prioritize the quality of our products, ensuring they are safe, effective, and enjoyable to use.

Our values

  • Cruelty-free formulas, always.
  • A shade range built for everyone.
  • Honest, professional-friendly pricing.

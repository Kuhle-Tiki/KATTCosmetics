Enquire to purchase

KATT Cosmetics does not process payments through this website. Fill in the form below with the product(s) you're interested in, and you'll see an instant estimate of price and availability. Our team will then contact you via your preferred method to confirm stock, pricing, and how to complete your purchase.

How it works

  1. Browse our product pages and note down what you're interested in.
  2. Submit the enquiry form below, or contact us directly via WhatsApp, email, or phone.
  3. Our team confirms stock, pricing, and delivery or collection options with you.
  4. Payment and delivery are arranged directly with our team, not through this website.

Enquiry questions

How is my estimate calculated?

We match the products you typed to our range and multiply each price by your quantity. Items without a listed price show "Price on request" and are quoted by our team.

Is there a discount for larger orders?

Yes. Ordering 10 or more of an item shows a 10% trade discount in your estimate. Makeup artists can also apply for a trade account.

Is the estimate final?

No. It is an estimate only. Our team confirms stock, final pricing and delivery or collection with you before any payment.

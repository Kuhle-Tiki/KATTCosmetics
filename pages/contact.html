Frequently asked questions

How do I buy a product?

This website does not process online payments. Browse our products, then contact us via WhatsApp, email or Instagram to confirm availability, pricing and delivery or collection.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping typically takes 3-5 business days within South Africa. International shipping times vary by destination.

What is your returns policy?

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.

Are your products cruelty-free?

Yes, all KATT Cosmetics products are cruelty-free and not tested on animals.
